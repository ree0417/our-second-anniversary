Our Love Story

Every beautiful love story has a beginning. This is ours.

25 JUNE 2024 · OUR FIRST MEETING

The Girl I Noticed From A Distance

We were both first-year varsity students, coming from writing our last paper for the first semester. You were a quiet, humble girl who hardly socialised. You didn't have friends around you, and you weren't the easiest person to approach. But somehow, from a distance, I could already see your humbleness. There was something special about you that made me want to know you better.

THE DAY I ASKED FOR YOUR NUMBER

"Go Get It From The Class Group"

I finally gathered the courage to ask for your number. Instead of giving it to me, you told me to get it from our class group chat. But I wanted you to type it yourself on my phone. And you did. Looking back, that little moment means so much because it became the beginning of our story. We started talking, and slowly, I got to know the beautiful person behind that quiet smile.

PATIENCE AND UNDERSTANDING

I Was Willing To Wait

When we started talking, you told me that you weren't ready to be in a relationship. I told you that I wasn't in a rush and that you could take your time. I didn't want to force anything. I wanted you to feel comfortable, to know me properly, and to make your own decision when you were ready. You were worth the wait.

13 OCTOBER 2024 at 20:06 · OUR ANNIVERSARY

The Day You Became Mine

Then came 13 October 2024 at 20:06, the day our story officially became ours. Today, two years later, I look back with gratitude. The girl I once noticed from a distance has become someone deeply important in my life. Thank you for loving me and for making me feel like those loved guys I used to see. With you, I get to experience that love too.

A message from my heart

To My Beautiful Girl ❤️

Some things deserve to be written down forever.

My Love,

Happy two years to us, my love. ❤️

Sometimes I think about how we first met on 25 June 2024. We were just two first-year students coming from our last paper for the semester, and I had no idea that the quiet, humble girl I noticed from a distance would one day become someone this important to me.

I still smile when I remember asking for your number and you telling me to get it from the class group chat. I insisted that you type it on my phone yourself, and you did. From that little moment, we started talking and slowly building something real.

When you told me you weren't ready for a relationship, I didn't want to rush you. I wanted you to have the time and freedom to choose me when you were ready. On 13 October 2024, you became mine, and today I am grateful that we are celebrating two years together.

You make me feel loved. You remind me that I, too, can experience the kind of love I used to see other people enjoying. I appreciate your heart, your humility, your quiet nature, and all the little ways you make my life feel warmer.

My intention is to keep choosing you and to keep you in my life for as long as God allows. I want us to grow together, support each other, celebrate our achievements, and face life's difficult moments as a team.

I look forward to our graduation day. One of my hopes is to officially introduce you to my family because you have become so meaningful to me. You have shown me qualities that make me want to keep building something beautiful with you.

I cannot promise that every day will be perfect, but I can promise to keep learning, listening, growing, praying, and showing up with love. May God guide us, protect what we are building, and give us the wisdom to love each other well.

Thank you for choosing us. Thank you for being yourself. Thank you for these two years of memories, laughter, love, and growth.

If I could go back to that day after our last paper, I would still want to meet you. I would still want to ask for your number. I would still want to get to know you.

And if God gives us the opportunity, I would love to keep choosing you through all the chapters that are still waiting for us.

Happy second anniversary, my favourite person. This is two years of us, but my heart hopes for many more.

Yours, today and for the days God gives us. ♡
